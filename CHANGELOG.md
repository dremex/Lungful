# Changelog

## 1.0.0

The first release.

- At 70% breath left the breath bar turns a faint yellow that deepens to full yellow by 50%. It then steps toward orange, full by 40%, and toward red, full by 30%, and starts flashing at 25%. The flashing speeds up as breath runs out, and from 10% the whole bar flashes fast.
- Pick a different red point, or let Lungful work it out from how long you've been under water.
- The fatigue bar in deep water gets the same colors. You can turn that off.
- Options are under Options > AddOns > Lungful, and `/lungful help` lists the commands. `/lungful test` plays a sped-up dive so you can see each stage.
